Materials

What Goes Into Every Happit Toy

🖍️

Washable, Non-toxic Dyes

All colouring materials use EN71-certified, water-soluble dyes with zero harmful solvents. Safe even if ingested in small amounts.

🪵

Child-safe Plastics & Wood

BPA-free plastics only. Wood pieces use FSC-certified timber with rounded edges and sanded surfaces — no splinters.

🧵

Soft, Hypoallergenic Fabrics

All fabric-based toys use OEKO-TEX certified materials, free from allergens, heavy metals and formaldehyde.

🔩

No Small Parts

Every piece in our 3+ range passes the choke-hazard test. For 5+ products, age-appropriate warning labels are always included.

📦

Eco Packaging

100% recyclable cardboard boxes with soy-based inks. No styrofoam, no plastic windows, no twist ties. Just the toy.

🌡️

Durability Tested

Drop tests, stress tests, and thermal tests ensure our toys survive the rough-and-tumble reality of kids' play.
