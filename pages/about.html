How it started

A Parent's Dream for Every Child

Happit was born out of a simple frustration — finding safe, truly fun toys for young kids that were also genuinely educational was harder than it should be. Too many toys were either boring or unsafe, with poor materials and zero thought for development.

We set out to change that. Working with child development specialists, safety engineers, and — most importantly — real kids and their parents, we created a range of toys that tick every box: safe, certified, beautiful, and irresistibly fun.

Today, Happit is trusted by over 10,000 families across India. Every product is carefully designed, rigorously tested, and sold exclusively through Amazon India so you always get authentic, quality-assured products with easy returns.
