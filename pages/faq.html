🛡️ Safety & Certifications
Are Happit toys BIS certified?▾
Yes. Every Happit toy is certified by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), which is the highest toy safety standard in India. Our certification covers material safety, mechanical safety, and age-appropriateness.
Are the paints and dyes safe if my child puts them in their mouth?▾
All colouring materials (paints, crayons, markers) used in Happit products are non-toxic, water-based, and certified to EN71-3 standards. While we don't encourage children to eat them, small amounts are not harmful. They are washable from skin and most fabrics.
Are there small parts that could be choking hazards?▾
All Happit toys for 3+ years pass the EU and IS choke-hazard tests — meaning no part is small enough to pose a risk to children in that age group. Products for 5+ years may contain smaller components and are clearly labelled.
Is your packaging eco-friendly?▾
Yes! All Happit packaging is made from recycled cardboard with soy-based inks. We use zero plastic windows, zero styrofoam, and zero plastic twist-ties. The box itself is a toy — we encourage kids to use it for creative projects!
